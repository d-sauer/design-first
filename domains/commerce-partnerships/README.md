# Commerce Partnerships

This domain encompasses commerce and partnership operations, including hosting public web pages with customer stores and ensuring seamless interaction with business partners through APIs.

## Capabilities

- **Public Storefront Hosting** - Hosting and managing public web pages for customer stores
- **Customer Store Management** - Managing customer store configurations and content
- **Partner API Integration** - Providing and managing APIs for partner interactions
- **Partner Relationship Management** - Ensuring and facilitating interactions with business partners
